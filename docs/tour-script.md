# Welcome and tour: draft script

The first time someone opens Home, a welcome dialog opens. It shows the owner's welcome message and offers a quick tour. The tour is a series of small cards. Each card points at the part of the screen it describes. A user sees the welcome dialog once, and can open it again from Settings.

{community} is the community's name. {owner} is the owner's username. {topic} is the name of one of the community's topics.

"Shown" says when a step appears. "Points at" says where its card sits. On a phone, a card is the width of the screen less 16px on each side, and sits below or above its target.

## 1. The welcome dialog

Shown: to everyone, the first time they open Home. The owner sees it after the welcome page. The dialog is in the middle of the screen, on a desktop and on a phone.

Heading: Welcome to {community}
Text: {community} runs on Sweet.
Then, when the owner has written one, the welcome message, under {owner}'s picture and name.
Then: Would you like a quick tour? You can start it again from Settings at any time.
Buttons: Not now, Show me around

## The tour

Each card has Back from the second card on, Skip tour, and Next. The last card a user sees has Done in place of Next. Each card shows its number out of the total, such as 2 of 7, at the right of its heading. The welcome dialog is not counted.

### 2. Posts

Shown: always.
Points at: the posts column on a desktop. The Posts icon in the top bar on a phone.

Heading: Posts
Desktop: This column is for posts. A post is a longer, lasting piece of writing that people can comment on.
Phone: Posts are here. A post is a longer, lasting piece of writing that people can comment on.
### 3. Chat

Shown: always.
Points at: the chat column on a desktop. The Chat icon in the top bar on a phone.

Heading: Chat
Desktop: This column is the chat. Everyone in {community} can read and write in it. Use it for quick messages.
Phone: Tap here to switch to the chat. Everyone in {community} can read and write in it. Use it for quick messages. The icon beside it switches back to posts.

### 4. The line between the columns

Shown: on a desktop only. A phone shows one column at a time.
Points at: the divider between the posts and the chat.

Heading: Resize the columns
Text: Drag this line to make one column wider. Drag it all the way to one side to hide a column. Click the strip that is left to bring that column back.

### 5. Topics

Shown: when the community has at least one topic.
Points at: the topic menu above the posts. On a phone, the tour switches to the posts first.

Heading: Topics
Text: A post can have a topic, such as {topic}. Choose a topic here to see only its posts. While a topic is chosen, anything you post goes into it. Subscribe to a topic to get a notification when someone posts in it.

### 6. Rooms

Shown: when the user may create rooms. On the Community page, the owner decides whether everyone may create rooms or only the owner and moderators.
Points at: Rooms in the side menu on a desktop. The Menu tab on a phone.

Heading: Rooms
Desktop: A room is a private space with its own posts and chat. Only the people in it can see it. You could make one to bring together a few people who share an interest. Create one with the plus button, then add people to it.
Phone: A room is a private space with its own posts and chat. Only the people in it can see it. You could make one to bring together a few people who share an interest. Your rooms are in the Menu, and you can create one there.
For the owner, the third sentence reads: You could make one for you and the moderators who help you run {community}, or for a few people who share an interest.

### 7. Conversations

Shown: always.
Points at: Conversations in the side menu on a desktop. The Menu tab on a phone.

Heading: Conversations
Desktop: A conversation is a private chat between you and one other person. Start one with the plus button, or with Message on the People page.
Phone: A conversation is a private chat between you and one other person. Your conversations are in the Menu. Start one there, or with Message on the People page.

### 8. Rules

Shown: to everyone except the owner, when the community has rules.
Points at: the Rules button in Home's top bar on a desktop. The Menu tab on a phone.

Heading: Rules
Desktop: You agreed to {community}'s rules when you joined. You can read them again here.
Phone: You agreed to {community}'s rules when you joined. You can read them again in the Menu.

### 9. The Community page

Shown: to the owner only.
Points at: Community in the side menu on a desktop. The Menu tab on a phone.

Heading: Running {community}
Desktop: The Community page is where you look after {community}. You can change its name and description, write its rules, add topics, and see posts and messages that members have reported. A shield marks any action or section that only you and your moderators can see.
Moderators help you look after {community}. You choose them on the People page, and you invite new people there too.
Phone: the same text, with "The Community page is in the Menu." as its first sentence.

### 10. Welcome message

Shown: to the owner only, last.
Points at: nothing. The card is in the middle of the screen.

Heading: Write a welcome message
Text: Everyone who joins sees your welcome message when they first open {community}. You might ask them to introduce themselves in an Introductions topic, to read the rules, or to say hello in the chat.
Buttons: Back, Later, Write welcome message. Write welcome message opens the Community page at the Welcome message card.

## The Welcome message card

On the Community page, after About this community and before Rules. It works like the Rules card, with the same editor.

Heading: Welcome message (with the shield: Only the owner and moderators see this)
Text: Everyone who joins sees this when they first open {community}. You might ask them to introduce themselves, to read the rules, or to say hello in the chat.
When it is empty: This community has no welcome message yet.
Placeholder in the editor: What newcomers read first…
Button: Write welcome message, or Edit welcome message once there is one.

## Settings

A card on Settings, after Interface mode.

Heading: Welcome
Text: Read {community}'s welcome message again, or take the tour.
Button: Show welcome

## Decided

The owner and moderators edit the welcome message, as they do the rules (Raphael, 2026-11-09).
